Blood Storage & Transport Guidelines

Important information for handling blood products safely

Temperature Control

Whole blood must be stored at 1-6°C. Red cells at 1-6°C, platelets at 20-24°C with agitation, and plasma at -18°C or below.

Transport Requirements

Use validated containers that maintain required temperatures. Monitor temperature during transport. Maximum transport time varies by product.

Expiration & Handling

Whole blood expires in 21-35 days. Platelets expire in 5 days. Never freeze whole blood or red cells. Always check expiration before use.
