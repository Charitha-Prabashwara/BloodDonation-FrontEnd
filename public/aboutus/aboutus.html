Our Origin

In 2015, a group of medical students at the University of Colombo witnessed a tragic event that would change their lives forever. A young mother in labor needed an emergency blood transfusion, but the hospital's supply of her rare blood type had run out. Despite frantic efforts to locate a donor, they were too late. This heartbreaking experience became the catalyst for what is now Sri Lanka's most comprehensive blood donation platform.

From Tragedy to Transformation

What began as a simple spreadsheet shared among university students quickly evolved into a nationwide movement. The founders realized that the problem wasn't a lack of willing donors, but rather the absence of an efficient system to connect them with those in need at critical moments.

Through tireless effort and innovative thinking, Blood Link developed a platform that now serves all nine provinces of Sri Lanka, partnering with major hospitals and blood banks to ensure no patient suffers due to lack of access to life-saving blood donations.

"Every drop of blood donated is a story of hope, a potential life saved. We're not just connecting donors with recipients - we're facilitating miracles."

— Dr. Sanjaya Perera, Medical Director
