Blood Donation Information

The person must fulfill several criteria to be accepted as a blood donor. These criteria are set forth to ensure the safety of the donor as well as the quality of donated blood.

Who Can Donate?

  • Age above 18 years and below 60 years
  • If previously donated, at least 4 months should have passed since last donation
  • Hemoglobin level should be more than 12g/dL (tested before donation)
  • Free from any serious disease condition or pregnancy
  • Should have valid identity proof
  • Free from "Risk Behaviors"

Waiting Periods

  • Minimum 56 days between whole blood donations
  • 7 days between platelet donations
  • 28 days between double red cell donations
  • 1 year after getting a tattoo or piercing (unless done with sterile procedure)

Why Some Can't Donate

  • Health Conditions: Certain illnesses, low iron levels, or recent surgeries
  • Risk Behaviors:
    • Homosexual activity (in some regions)
    • Sex workers and their clients
    • Drug addicts
    • Having multiple sexual partners
  • Recent Travel: To areas with malaria risk
  • Medications: Some prescriptions may defer donation

How to Prepare

Before Donation:

  • Get a good night's sleep
  • Eat iron-rich foods in preceding days
  • Drink extra fluids (especially water)
  • Eat a healthy meal before donating
  • Avoid fatty foods before donation

After Donation:

  • Rest for 10-15 minutes
  • Drink plenty of fluids
  • Avoid strenuous activity for 5 hours
  • Keep the bandage on for several hours
  • Eat iron-rich foods to replenish

Types of Donors

Voluntary

Donate for others without expecting benefit (safest blood)

Replacement

Donate to replace units used for friends/family

Paid

Receive payment for donation (higher risk)

Directed

Donate specifically for a patient's requirement

The Donation Process

Donating blood is simple and safe. Here's what you can expect when you donate.

1

Registration

You'll complete a donor registration form with your personal information and health history.

2

Health Screening

A staff member will check your temperature, pulse, blood pressure and hemoglobin level.

3

Donation

The actual blood donation takes about 8-10 minutes. You'll donate about 1 pint of blood.

4

Refreshment

After donating, you'll enjoy snacks and drinks while you rest for 10-15 minutes.
